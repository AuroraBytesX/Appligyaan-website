Curriculum & Tracks

Practical Technical Courses for Real-World Work

Choose a career-oriented track matched to your engineering focus. Both programs prioritize structured laboratory practice, industrial standards, and mentor guidance.

Hardware & IoT Track APPLI-RES-101

Robotics and Embedded Systems

Gain practical proficiency in electronic prototyping and embedded control. Wire, program, and test autonomous circuits using Arduino Uno and ESP32 with real sensors, actuators, and communication modules.

Core Modules & Laboratory Work
  • Robotics architecture & circuit fundamentals
  • Arduino Uno & ESP32 microcontroller programming
  • Ultrasonic, IR, temperature & motion sensor interfacing
  • DC motors, servo motors, relays & motor driver circuits
  • Embedded C/C++, PWM, ADC & serial debugging
  • IoT communication & smart automation capstone project
Target Learners School STEM, Diploma & B.Tech Students
Training Format Hardware Bench Practice

Civil & Architectural Track APPLI-CAD-102

Building Drawing using AutoCAD 2D

Master civil drafting standards, architectural layouts, and municipal sanction drawings. Learn to produce clean, submission-ready blueprints conforming to municipal building bye-laws.

Core Modules & Drafting Skills
  • AutoCAD 2D setup, commands & layer management
  • Residential & commercial architectural floor plans
  • Municipal sanction drawings & local building bye-laws
  • Structural layouts, column grids & foundation details
  • Sectional views, elevations & door-window schedules
  • Scale plotting, sheet layouts & client blueprint production
Target Learners Civil, Architecture & ITI Draftspersons
Training Format CAD Workstation Sessions

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information about course curriculum, prerequisites, admissions, and campus visits at Appligyaan Institute, Barasat.

Appligyaan Institute offers two specialized technical training programs: (1) Robotics and Embedded Systems, covering microcontrollers (Arduino Uno, ESP32), sensor interfacing, actuators, motor control, and embedded C/C++ programming; and (2) Building Drawing using AutoCAD 2D, focusing on civil drafting standards, architectural floor plans, structural layouts, and municipal sanction drawings.
Our campus is located at 12 No. Railgate, Lichutala, Barasat, West Bengal – 700124, India, just minutes from Barasat Railway Station. The official website is www.appligyaan.in, and you can view our location on Google Maps (CID: 13740462140244696701).
You can contact our admissions desk directly via call or WhatsApp at +91 72782 84166 and +91 70440 26278. Inquiries are handled promptly to share course syllabi, fee schedules, and batch timings.
This course is designed for civil engineering students (Diploma and B.Tech), ITI draftspersons, architectural assistants, site supervisors, and contractors who want to master CAD blueprints, structural detailing, and municipal sanction submission standards.
No prior coding or circuit background is required. The Robotics and Embedded Systems curriculum starts from the basics of electronic components and breadboarding before advancing into microcontroller logic and C/C++ programming.
Appligyaan Institute does not track or sell student data. All admissions communications are handled directly through official WhatsApp channels.
